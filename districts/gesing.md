## history

- name from **bambu / gesing** that once dominated the hills — agreed by villagers and tokoh
- ~1930: two *Kelian Banjar Dinas* (Tengah · Delod Pempatan), ~150 KK
- **1954**: ratified as *Desa Dinas Gesing* (Bupati Ida Bagus Mahadewa era)
- first *perbekel* Wayan Kumita (1954–1967) — SD Gesing I (1956), Jembatan Tukad Cangkup, Pura Dalem / Subak
- later memory: Monumen Perjuangan Anjasmara in the desa

## places & character

highland village on the Banjar–Munduk belt — cooler air, mist, coffee/clove dryland, edge of protected forest toward [[Sanghyang]] / Batukaru massif. Munduk (tourism) sits next door; the south coast tourist machine is hours away. roads are steep and often damaged; trails run into Batuka forest. quiet by Bali standards — that is the point.

## why gesing (for cyber valley)

| pull | why it matters |
|------|----------------|
| climate | cool highland — workable off-grid life without AC culture |
| soils | volcanic andosol; real fertility if remediated from plantation chemistry |
| price / remoteness | remote land bought early; infra talk (roads / north-bali airport lore) as upside, not dependency |
| village legitimacy | PT PMA domiciled here — neighbors, banjar, and permits are local facts |

| arrival | visitors hit village edge first — [[core]] + [[bridge]] are the conversion surface |

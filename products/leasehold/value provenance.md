# value provenance

Every figure in the [[hak sewa template]] has one of three origins: a law that leaves no choice, a decision of the Company, or an engineering benchmark adopted as the minimum. This page names the origin of each, so a buyer reads the numbers the way the Company set them. A question this table leaves open goes to support in a direct message; the answer is added here.

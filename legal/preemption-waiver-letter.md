# Waiver of pre-emption — side letter

Draft · annexed to the deed of amendment of the [[articles-of-association]], outside its body

> Status. A standalone declaration signed by both existing shareholders and handed to the notary together with the amendment. It stays out of the Articles: the Articles carry the general rule, this letter disapplies it once, for a named tranche and a named purpose.

Why it exists: [[ceo-terms-decisions]] Д2. The option granted to the Director is satisfied by an issue of new shares. [[articles-of-association]] Article 4(3) gives shareholders on the register a pre-emptive right to subscribe for newly issued shares within 14 days, in proportion to their holdings. Exercised over this tranche, that right would absorb the very shares being issued to satisfy the option, and the option would fail while the Articles were fully observed.

## Operative text

> SURAT PERNYATAAN PELEPASAN HAK TERLEBIH DAHULU
>
> Yang bertanda tangan di bawah ini:
>
> 1. ALISA VOINOVA, pemegang 3.940 saham dalam PT. CYBER VALLEY ESTATE;
> 2. DMITRY STARODUBTSEV, pemegang 4.020 saham dalam PT. CYBER VALLEY ESTATE;
>
> dengan ini menyatakan, secara tegas dan tidak dapat ditarik kembali, melepaskan hak terlebih dahulu untuk mengambil bagian atas saham sebagaimana dimaksud dalam Pasal 4 ayat (3) Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, semata-mata sepanjang menyangkut penerbitan sebanyak-banyaknya 1.608 (seribu enam ratus delapan) saham baru kepada OLEKSANDR FEDOROV dalam rangka pelaksanaan hak opsi yang diberikan kepadanya, yang diterbitkan dalam tiga tahap masing-masing sebanyak 402, 402, dan 804 saham.
>
> Pelepasan ini terbatas pada saham dan tujuan tersebut di atas, tidak berlaku bagi penerbitan saham lainnya, dan tidak mengurangi hak Para Pemegang Saham berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar maupun Perjanjian Pemegang Saham dalam hal apapun selain yang dinyatakan di sini.
>
> Pernyataan ini merupakan lampiran dari akta perubahan Anggaran Dasar dan berlaku sejak tanggal ditandatangani.

> DECLARATION OF WAIVER OF PRE-EMPTIVE RIGHTS
>
> The undersigned:
>
> 1. ALISA VOINOVA, holder of 3,940 shares in PT. CYBER VALLEY ESTATE;
> 2. DMITRY STARODUBTSEV, holder of 4,020 shares in PT. CYBER VALLEY ESTATE;
>
> hereby expressly and irrevocably waive the pre-emptive right to subscribe for shares under Article 4(3) of the Articles of Association of the Company, solely in respect of the issue of up to 1,608 (one thousand six hundred and eight) new shares to OLEKSANDR FEDOROV in satisfaction of the option granted to him, issued in three tranches of 402, 402 and 804 shares respectively.
>
> This waiver is limited to those shares and that purpose, does not extend to any other issue of shares, and does not diminish the rights of the Shareholders under the Articles of Association or the Shareholders' Agreement in any respect other than as stated here.
>
> This declaration is annexed to the deed of amendment of the Articles of Association and takes effect on the date of signature.

## If the option is satisfied by sale instead of issue

Under variant (в) of [[ceo-terms-decisions]] Д2 the shares come from an existing shareholder rather than from an issue. The letter then waives a different provision: Article 7(3) and 7(4) of the Articles (the duty to offer to the other shareholder first, with 30 days to take them up) and the Matching Right under Article 1 of the [[shareholders-agreement]]. The tranche numbers, the limitation to purpose and the annexation stay as above. The waiver is signed by the shareholder who is not selling.

## Notes

- Signed by both existing shareholders, since Article 4(3) gives the right to each of them.
- Handed to the notary with the amendment; not recited in the body of the Articles.
- Covers up to 1,608 shares in total across three tranches, so one letter serves all three exercises and no further waiver is needed at the second and third.
- The Director's own accession under Article 5(2) is a separate document and is unaffected.

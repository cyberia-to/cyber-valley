# CEO Agreement — Terms and Theses

Translated from the Russian working document `Условия и тезисы для договора с CEO`.

> Status. Negotiating position for the CEO contract, written to be handed to counsel. Nothing here is executed. Blanks in the source (`______`) and the author's inline queries are preserved as they stand.

Part of the [[dzin]] corporate pack. Reads together with [[revenue-model]] (the commission arithmetic), [[shareholders-agreement]] (the equity that the same person holds as a Shareholder) and [[articles-of-association]] (the authority the same person holds as Director).

## 1. Role and area of responsibility of the CEO

The CEO is responsible for the strategic and operational development of Cyber Valley and drives growth in the value of the holding through team management, development of the territory, attraction of investment, partners and sub-projects, and the launch of infrastructure and commercial lines of business.

The CEO's results and KPI are to be assessed against the resources the company actually provided: the approved budget, access to land, permits, the team, and timely corporate decisions.

## 2. CEO remuneration

The CEO receives:

- commissions and other payments provided for in the section [[revenue-model|Cyber Valley revenue and compensation model]];
- a fixed salary in an agreed amount, provided that ______
- dividends on the shares of the company held by the CEO;
- KPI bonuses under a separately approved KPI table; (source query: Dima?)
- reimbursement of agreed expenses incurred in the interests of the company.

The CEO's commissions are to be documented in a separate Compensation Schedule with a clear definition of the calculation base, the moment the right to a commission arises, the payment procedure under instalment sales, referral attribution, and the survival of already-earned commissions after the CEO's term of office ends.

## 3. Equity and options

On taking office the CEO acquires 1% of the shares of the company.

The CEO receives an option right to acquire additional shares:

- 5% — within 2 years;
- 5% — within 5 years;
- 10% — within 10 years.

The option strike is assumed on a company valuation of USD 4,000,000: 1% = USD 40,000.

The option rights are to be secured in law as exercisable rights of the CEO, defining the exercise period, the conditions under which the option survives the end of the CEO's term of office, anti-dilution protection, and the mechanism on a Change of Control.

## 4. Sale of the CEO's stake

The CEO may sell the shares he holds. Existing shareholders have a priority right to buy on the same terms, and the procedure for that right must avoid unreasonably blocking the sale.

On a sale of a controlling stake or of the company to a third party, the CEO is to have the right to join the sale with his own stake on comparable terms (tag-along), where applicable to the structure of the company.

## 5. The CEO's own projects — DevelopmentCo and SPV

The CEO may participate, directly or through companies and SPVs he owns, in individual projects on the territory of Cyber Valley, including for example:

- SPA and wellness;
- restaurant;
- construction of villas for rent;
- bathhouse;
- the event line of business;
- glamping;
- production;
- farms and plantations;
- sports and wellness activities;
- other agreed projects.

Such projects are permitted subject to disclosure of the potential conflict of interest and are documented by separate agreements between LandCo / Cyber Valley and the relevant CEO company or SPV on transparent, arm's-length terms.

## 6. Authority Matrix

A CEO Authority Matrix is to be attached to the agreement, clearly defining the CEO's powers and the matters that require separate approval.

Within the approved budget the CEO acts alone to:

- take ordinary operational decisions;
- hire and dismiss employees;
- conclude agreements and approve expenditure up to an agreed limit;
- work with contractors and suppliers;
- conclude standard agreements within the approved project model;
- manage the operational activity of the company.

Separate approval of the Commissioner and shareholders is required, in particular, for:

- transactions above the established limit;
- sale or pledge of material land assets;
- material bank loans and guarantees;
- issue of new shares;
- major construction and investment commitments;
- material related-party transactions;
- other Reserved Matters agreed separately by the parties.

## 7. Protection against obligations arising before the CEO's appointment

The CEO / Director bears no contractual liability to the company or the shareholders for acts, obligations, breaches or circumstances arising before the date of his appointment, unless the CEO personally participated in them.

The company and the shareholders confirm disclosure to the CEO of all known material:

- debts and financial obligations;
- agreements, MoUs, side agreements and material oral arrangements;
- guarantees and suretyships;
- judicial and administrative proceedings;
- tax obligations;
- land disputes and encumbrances;
- material liabilities;
- related-party arrangements;
- other circumstances capable of materially affecting the company or the CEO's liability.

## 8. Indemnification and legal defence

The company and, where possible, a financially sound key shareholder provide indemnification, defence and hold-harmless cover to the CEO to the maximum extent permitted by Indonesian law in respect of:

- circumstances and obligations arising before the CEO's appointment;
- concealed or undisclosed obligations;
- inaccurate representations and warranties of the company or shareholders;
- acts of previous Directors and management;
- claims against the CEO arising from the good-faith performance of his powers.

The costs of the CEO's legal defence are, where possible, to be paid or advanced by the company from the moment the relevant claim arises, rather than reimbursed to the CEO after the fact.

## 9. D&O insurance

Directors & Officers Liability Insurance is to be provided for, if such cover is available on reasonable commercial terms, including legal defence and run-off cover for acts of the CEO committed during his term of office.

## 10. Right to rely on professionals

The CEO may rely in good faith on the professional opinions and recommendations of lawyers, accountants, auditors, tax advisers, architects, engineers, land consultants and other engaged specialists within their competence.

## 11. Information rights

The CEO is to have timely and complete access to the information needed to perform his duties, including:

- bank accounts and banking documents;
- accounting and financial records;
- agreements;
- corporate records;
- land documents;
- tax records;
- employee records;
- material correspondence of the previous management.

The CEO bears no contractual liability to the company or shareholders for the consequences of information or obligations knowingly concealed from him by the company, the shareholders or the previous management.

## 12. Right to refuse unlawful or unauthorised acts

The CEO may refuse to carry out instructions or sign transactions that show reasonable indicia of illegality or of missing corporate authority, or that create unjustified risk of personal liability for the CEO. Material shareholder instructions going beyond ordinary operational activity are to be recorded in writing.

## 13. Conflict of interest and related-party transactions

A clear procedure is to be established for disclosing and approving conflicts of interest and related-party transactions, including transactions between LandCo, DevelopmentCo/SPV and companies in which the CEO is a director, shareholder or otherwise an interested person.

## 14. Termination and removal

The agreement is to define clearly:

- the notice period;
- the procedure for resignation and removal as Director;
- payment of earned salary, commissions and accrued bonuses;
- the fate of equity and options;
- Good Leaver / Bad Leaver;
- a closed list of circumstances constituting Cause.

Strategic disagreement, loss of confidence absent a proven material breach, and failure to meet KPI resulting from the company's failure to provide the necessary resources are each, on their own, outside the definition of Cause.

## 15. Protection of the CEO on termination without Cause

If the company or the shareholders terminate the CEO's powers without Cause, the CEO has the right, at his election, to:

- retain his entire stake and remain a shareholder of the company; or
- receive a buyout of his stake at Fair Market Value as at the date of exit, determined by independent valuation taking into account the market value of the land, projects and other material assets of the company less real liabilities. In any event the amount is to be no less than the sum the CEO invested in the company.

If the CEO elects the buyout, the corresponding consideration is to be paid in full or reliably secured as part of the procedure terminating his powers. Counsel is to propose the most reliable mechanism for this principle that is permissible under Indonesian law.

The buyout of the stake stands separately from the CEO's severance / termination compensation.

## 16. Protection of equity and company value

Protection of the CEO is to be provided against:

- unjustified dilution of his stake;
- artificial reduction of the value of the company ahead of a buyout or dismissal;
- non-arm's-length transfer of land, projects and other assets to related companies;
- creation of artificial obligations or debt;
- other acts aimed at circumventing the CEO's economic rights.

## 17. Change of control — sale of the company or of material assets

On a sale of the company, of a controlling stake, of a significant part of the land assets or of a key project, the CEO's rights are to be defined, including:

- tag-along;
- retention or exercise of equity and options;
- possible acceleration of options;
- payment of accrued commissions and bonuses;
- cash-out on comparable economic terms, where applicable.

## 18. Survival of earned commissions after departure

Commissions and other economic rights of the CEO that were earned, or that arise from transactions, clients, investors or projects introduced by the CEO before the end of his term of office, survive and are paid after his departure in accordance with the economic model originally agreed.

## 19. Protection after termination

Indemnification, legal defence and applicable D&O cover continue to protect the CEO after departure in respect of acts and decisions taken during his term of office.

## 20. Confidentiality and non-compete

The CEO observes reasonable confidentiality obligations. Any non-compete or non-solicitation restrictions are to be clearly limited in duration, territory and subject matter, and are to leave the CEO free to carry on entrepreneurial activity after the end of the engagement.

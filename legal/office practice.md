# office practice

What the Kantor Pertanahan of Buleleng and counsel answer in writing about the Hak Pakai and Hak Milik tracks. [[hak sewa template]] 12.1.13 obliges the Landowner to obtain these answers, at its own cost, before the first Hak Pakai Notice; Part 12 is then conformed to them. The answers come through the Company's counsel; until a row carries one, the track runs on the template's defaults and the office's order governs where it differs (12.1.1).

## the eight questions

| # | question | answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the order of *pemecahan sertifikat*, *pelepasan hak* and the grant of Hak Pakai | awaiting the office |
| 2 | the construction stage at which a grant application is accepted — SLF, or already at PBG | awaiting the office |
| 3 | the documents the office requires for the track | awaiting the office |
| 4 | the spatial-plan treatment of the Estate for a split residential parcel | awaiting the office |
| 5 | the current value, area and parcel limits for a foreign holder — Kepmen ATR/BPN 1241/SK-HK.02/IX/2022 today | awaiting the office |
| 6 | whether the office accepts the irrevocable power of attorney of 12.1.8 | awaiting counsel |
| 7 | what evidences *rumah tinggal* — PBG, a village head's *keterangan*, or either | awaiting the office |
| 8 | the covenant mechanism of 12.1.14 — what the acquisition deed may carry | awaiting counsel |

Each answer lands here with its date and the instrument it rests on; the page is the register's record of the office's practice. A conflict between an answer and the template's words resolves for the answer — 12.1.13 conforms the Part.

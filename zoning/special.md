# Special

**Strictly designated purpose.** One parcel, one declared function. Change of use fires reversion.

## Examples

- school / education campus
- clinic / hospital
- tech production, labs, clean workshops
- construction infrastructure and staging yards
- cowork / common house when locked as community service (not open retail)

## Allowed

Only the use written in the deed. Accessory space that serves that use (staff room, storage, small canteen for the facility).

## Not this zone

- open residential + small commerce → [[residential]]
- tourist / retail magnets → [[commercial]]
- agroforestry / conservation → [[forestry]]
- shared infra and public realm → [[commons]]

## Deal posture

Often near-zero or subsidized leasehold plus participation after breakeven. Deliberately not a cash engine: returns come as uplift on adjacent [[residential]] and legitimacy for SETTLE. Land-for-equity and JV for aligned ventures (labs, production). Deed restrictions + reversion on function change.

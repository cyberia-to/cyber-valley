# Forestry

Working and sacred landscape: **conservation, agroforestry, permaculture.** The amenity that prices the built city.

## Allowed

- conservation stands, watershed, biodiversity corridors
- agroforestry, permaculture, nursery, livestock, aquaculture under estate rules
- light trails and observation that do not convert canopy to footprint
- carbon / biodiversity credit layers where they do not break the ecology brief

## Not this zone

- villas and shops → [[residential]] / [[commercial]]
- institutions and yards → [[special]]
- roads, parking, plazas as primary use → [[commons]]

## Deal posture

Permanent holding bias. Farm operators: annual leases + participation, or operator agreements with no land rights transferred. Conservation parcels: no sale. Output feeds estate F&B — soil to table. A plot next to managed forest and productive land commands a premium over bare subdivision; the unsold land sells the sold land.

## Fit with law

Aligns with green / agricultural protective intent (*kawasan pertanian*, *lindung*) where construction is limited or banned. Estate forestry is stricter than “plant a few trees on a villa lot.”

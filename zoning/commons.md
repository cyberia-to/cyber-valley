# Commons

Shared city body: **infra, trails, plazas.** Owned and controlled by the estate — not subdivided into private homes.

## Allowed

- roads, mobility hub, parking, gates, utilities (energy, water, connectivity, waste)
- trails, plazas, public clearings, shared gathering ground
- sensors and nodes that serve the whole settlement

## Not this zone

- private residences → [[residential]]
- commercial venues as primary use → [[commercial]]
- locked single-purpose institutions → [[special]]
- production / conservation canopy → [[forestry]]

## Deal posture

**Control layer.** Capex from early waves; ownership stays with PT PMA. Ops on fee contracts — no equity giveaway of the pipes. Perpetual transfer of infrastructure assets prohibited.

Parking slots are the only sellable micro-asset here (motorbike / car leases): cash without burning buildable residential land. Vehicles stop at the perimeter hub; the core stays car-free — silence and air as product.

## Fit with law

Closest to red / public-infrastructure intent: the city’s shared systems, not speculative lots.

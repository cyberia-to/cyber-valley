# Commercial

**Commercial activities only** — spa, market, restaurant, retail, hospitality. Demand engine of the city; not a private-home zone.

## Instruments

| | |
|--|--|
| Default | [[leasehold upfront]] · [[annual leasehold]] |
| Rare | [[joint venture]] — when the venue is an anchor the estate wants held with the operator |

## City obligation

**10%** of activity revenue to the city. Drops to **5%** after **autonomy certification** — the parcel / venue is a net exporter of energy, water, and food.

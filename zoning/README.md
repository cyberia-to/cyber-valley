## Estate zones

Legal envelope here is wide (yellow *permukiman* + *pariwisata* scope). These five types are the estate rule on top.

| zone | rule |
|------|------|
| [[residential]] | most flexible — self-live without 10% city obligation; cash in advance (no yearly leasehold) |
| [[commercial]] | commercial only — upfront / yearly leasehold; 10% → 5% after autonomy cert; JV rare |
| [[special]] | strict designated purpose |
| [[forestry]] | conservation · agroforestry · permaculture |
| [[commons]] | infra · trails · plazas |

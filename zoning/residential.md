# Residential

The **most flexible** zone in the city. Living, working, small commerce, guest stay — as long as it fits the tone. One feature, one antifeature.

| | |
|--|--|
| **Feature** | Self-living without the **10% city obligation** — live here as home; the area tax on city activity does not apply to private dwelling |
| **Antifeature** | **No yearly leasehold** — cash in advance only ([[leasehold upfront|A]], [[hak pakai|D]], [[hak milik|E]]). Instrument B does not open on this zone |

Homes first; commercial on the same plot is fine when quiet nights and the sacred core stay intact.

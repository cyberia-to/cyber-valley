# part II — festival ground rental

## 2. the ground

| parameter | value |
|---|---|
| total zone area | 2 ha |
| occupied by structures | ~0.1 ha |
| roads and access | ~0.1 ha |
| usable area | ~1.8 ha (180 are) |
| comfortable capacity | 150–200 people |
| power | 30 kW, expandable |
| infrastructure | showers, toilets, internet, common areas |
| rain-day covered reserve | dome |
| equipment | plasma screen, sound, moving lights, professional laser, microphones |

## 3. price: 200 people, 5 days

| position | usd | comment |
|---|---|---|
| ground rental, 3 festival days | 4,000 | base rate |
| setup + teardown (2 × 500) | 1,000 | load-in and strike |
| technical package | 1,200 | plasma, sound, lights, laser, mics, dome |
| environmental fee (non-refundable) | 800 | site restoration fund |
| banjar package (security + parking, 5 days) | 1,000–1,500 | pass-through, set by the banjar |
| contract total | 8,000–8,500 | |
| deposit (refundable) | 2,000 | held against damage |
| organizer wire total | 10,000–10,500 | 2,000 returned after teardown |

8,000 / 200 / 3 ≈ $13 per person per night — cheaper than the low end of hotel per-pax rates, on multiples of the floor area.

outside the package: electricity above 30 kW — $800 per extra 30 kW block, only after technical review; extension beyond 5 days — $500/day; over 200 people — +$20 per extra head, because load on toilets and ground rises non-linearly.

## 4. mandatory contract terms

1. two-sided handover act before load-in and after strike, with photo documentation.
2. liability insurance — on the organizer.
3. sound curfew, agreed with the banjar in writing.
4. headcount is fixed by number; overage is billed.
5. trash removal is the organizer's responsibility, or it is held from the deposit.
6. technical audit of the connection for requests above 30 kW — before signing.
7. 50% prepayment on date booking, balance and deposit — 14 days before arrival.

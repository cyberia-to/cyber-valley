# lease pricer

interactive pricer for ground-lease deals: leasehold economics = relativity × freehold; whatever the premium leaves on the table returns as rent at a risk-adjusted rate. the review clause pulls rent back to X% of current land value when land outruns the index — the protection [[development]] instrument B needs when g > CPI. long-term value preservation of the rent stream itself is the [[century index]].

the model: leasehold economics = k × freehold; the premium takes p of it upfront, the remainder returns as CPI-indexed rent discounted at r = r_base + spread·(1−p) — a smaller premium shifts value into the rent stream and raises the rate, pricing the tenant's default risk. review every N years resets rent to max(indexed path, X% × market freehold), where X defaults to the initial yield: the lessor keeps the same position in the land forever.

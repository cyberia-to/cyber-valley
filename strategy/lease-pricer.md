# lease pricer

interactive pricer for ground-lease deals. start from the lease value at full prepayment: at least 30% is paid at signing, the rest converts into an annual schedule that compounds three costs — the cost of money (discount), the money index ([[century index]]), and real land growth above it. pricing for [[development]] instrument B.

the model: V is the lease value at full prepayment. the upfront share p (30% minimum) is paid at signing; the remainder returns as a schedule growing at the combined rate G = (1+cx)(1+g)−1 and discounted at r = money + 4%·(1−p): the financing schedule prices the deferred share like LTV — pay more now, borrow cheaper. the three costs compound without double counting — inflation lives inside CX once, g is real growth above it; the rent keeps its share of the land by construction.

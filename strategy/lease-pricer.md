# lease pricer

interactive pricer for ground-lease deals. start from the lease value at full prepayment: at least 30% is paid at signing, the rest converts into annual rent at the discount rate, growing per the [[century index]] with expected land growth baked into the growth rate. pricing for [[development]] instrument B.

the model: V is the lease value at full prepayment. the upfront share p (30% minimum) is paid at signing; the remainder returns as rent growing at rate i and discounted at r: rent₁ = (1−p)·V / Σ (1+i)^(t−1)/(1+r)^t. expected land appreciation is priced into i rather than a review clause — set i to what the land is expected to do and the rent keeps its share of the land.

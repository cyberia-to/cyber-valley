# hak milik

freehold transfer of the parcel — instrument E of the [[development|strategy]], open to Indonesian citizens only.

the WNI ownership channel: hak milik is full ownership under Indonesian law, and only an individual warga negara Indonesia can hold it — no foreigners, no companies (PT PMA itself holds HGB, not hak milik). transfer by AJB before a PPAT, payment 100% at transfer, price on the same public wave curve as instrument A.

what the deal carries:

- full freehold title registered to the buyer
- the [[zoning/system|zoning code]] follows the land as recorded covenants — footprint caps, species density, pollution policies
- keeps the desa share of ownership strong — the tone guardrail that anchors the city in its village

for whom: Indonesian buyers and long-horizon local partners — the channel that makes cyber valley a mixed city, not an expat enclave.

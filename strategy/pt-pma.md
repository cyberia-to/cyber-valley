# PT PMA equity

investment in the city company — instrument **F** of the [[development|strategy]]. Not a plot: a share of the OpCo that holds the land rights, infra, and recurring streams.

## Cap table grain

| | |
|--|--|
| **Issued** | **8,040** shares |
| **Minimum subscription** | **4** shares |
| Payment | 100% at subscription |

One share = `1 / 8040` of the company. Floor of four keeps the register usable and blocks dust holdings.

## What it is / is not

- **Is:** equity in PT PMA — claim on the machine (leases, tariffs, participation, exit events per [[shareholders agreement]])
- **Is not:** a residential or commercial parcel (those are A–E); not a JV OpCo stake for a single venue (that is [[joint venture|C]])

Cash waterfall and shareholder rights live in the SHA — strategy only states that this channel exists and the grain is 8040 / min 4.

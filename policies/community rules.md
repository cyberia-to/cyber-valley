## 2. adat

the estate lives inside the customs of Desa Gesing. the Landowner records them per district before the first signing there and keeps the record current with the *desa adat*:

| item | record |
|---|---|
| *desa adat* and *banjar* | name; the *awig-awig* articles on land use, building and ceremony |
| *subak* | channels crossing the district; upkeep duty; water-sharing rule |
| ceremony | processional routes and temple approaches; the days they are used |
| contribution | the resident's contribution, in kind or money, and when it is due |

each item is shown to the holder at signing under 13.3 of [[hak sewa template]].

## 3. keeping the rules

a first breach of a standard is put right at the holder's cost within the cure period of [[hak sewa template]] 16.1. the same breach repeated within 12 months of that cure is a material default on the second notice.

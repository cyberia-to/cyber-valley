# design code

what is built on a plot and how. it binds every holder, every track and every zone of [[cyber valley]], and every assignee. the city and its residents amend it by proposal and vote under [[hak sewa template]] 10.5; a completed building keeps the rule it was built under unless an amendment improves its position. the reasoning behind each number is on [[zoning/system|zoning system]].

## 1. footprint

| parameter | value |
|---|---|
| ground footprint | 10% of the plot area |
| floor area | twice the footprint where two floors are used: 20% of the plot area |
| basement | counts as a floor where it rises above natural grade |

a plot of 1,000 m² carries 100 m² of footprint and up to 200 m² of floor area. the figure is fixed per plot by its area on the estate map.

## 2. garden and water

| element | share of the plot |
|---|---|
| garden — herbs, shrubs, trees, food beds | at least [70]% |
| pond | at least [3]% and at least [10] m², 2 m deep at the centre with gently sloping banks, for swimming and for edible aquaculture; every plot has one |
| paths and terraces | the remainder |

## 3. light balance

all sunlight falling on the plot is allocated across these receivers:

| receiver | minimum | maximum |
|---|---|---|
| light absorbers — photovoltaics, heat collectors | 1% | 10% |
| water bodies | 1% | 10% |
| transparent surfaces — greenhouses | 2% | 10% |
| lawn or ground cover | — | 5% |
| herbs or shrubs | 30% | — |
| low trees or canopy | 40% | — |

## 4. biodiversity

at least [60] plant species per plot, of which at least [30] are food or medicine, counted at SLF and at each SLF renewal. species on the estate's invasive list, published in the register, count for nothing.

## 5. built form

| element | rule | source |
|---|---|---|
| height | two storeys above natural grade, 15 m at most | Bali provincial height limit |
| springs | 15 m buffer around every spring, left in native cover | [[water policy]] |
| roofs | rain-collecting surfaces food-grade or stainless steel; the first rinse of each shower washes the roof into the garden, the rest fills the tank; all rain caught on the plot belongs to the plot | [[water policy]] |
| materials | natural and low-carbon: timber, bamboo, stone, lime, Roman cement, rammed earth, ferrocement; any colour | [[air policy]] |
| paints and finishes | water-based, zero-VOC and lead-free; natural oils, waxes and lime washes | [[air policy]] |
| prohibited materials | asbestos, lead-based paint, solvent-based coatings, PVC in potable lines, treated timber with arsenic or chromium | [[air policy]], [[water policy]] |
| lighting | main outdoor light 2200 K or warmer, shielded downward; decorative neon and RGB light shielded and off by 23:00 | [[light policy]] |
| fences | living hedges and open fences with passages for wildlife | biodiversity, section 4 |

## 6. water and wastewater

| system | rule |
|---|---|
| supply | PPR pipes; rainwater tanks sized under [[annex f utilities access mobility\|Annex F (Utilities, Access and Mobility)]] |
| blackwater | a biodigester on every plot; its gas cooks, its digestate feeds trees |
| greywater | treated on the plot to BOD below 30 mg/L before it returns to the soil |
| household products | soaps, detergents, cleaners and cosmetics that break down in soil within 5 months |

## 7. energy

each plot makes its own energy and stays off the public grid. the sources combine to suit a cloud-forest site:

| source | where it fits |
|---|---|
| photovoltaics | within the absorber band of section 3 |
| micro-hydro | on a plot a stream crosses, within the spring buffer rule |
| small wind | on ridges and open slopes |
| biogas | from the plot's biodigester, for cooking |
| batteries | sized to carry the plot through [3] days of cloud |
| generator | backup only, under [[air policy]] |

## 8. waste

organic waste becomes soil on the plot under [[compost]]. glass, metal, paper and plastic leave clean and sorted for the hub under [[recycling]].

## 9. public rules

provincial and Buleleng rules on height, architecture, slope and ravine setbacks, water catchment, *sempadan sungai*, *subak* and *jalan desa* apply alongside this code. where a public rule is stricter, it governs.

## 10. measurement and publication

| duty | frequency | who pays | published |
|---|---|---|---|
| discharge test | quarterly | holder | ledger and register |
| soil test | annual | holder | ledger and register |
| species count | at SLF and each SLF renewal | holder | register |

each result is published with the plot number and without the holder's name. two periods in a row without a published result count as a design code breach for the assignment fee under [[hak sewa template]] 4.5, and as a default under 16.1 of that deed once the notice period passes.

## 11. district balance

the register publishes for each district its total footprint and its light balance. unused footprint may pass to another plot of the same district from wave 2, against a settlement fee under [[hak sewa template]] 10.4, where the district stays within 10% footprint and every light-balance minimum.

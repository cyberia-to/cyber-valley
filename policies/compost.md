# compost

every plot turns its organic waste into soil. [[soil policy]] makes it a duty; this page is the method.

## what goes in

| in | kitchen scraps · garden cuttings · leaves · manure of herbivores · coffee grounds · paper towels · wood ash · biochar |
|---|---|
| out | meat and bones · dairy · oil · glass · metal · plastic · coated paper · pet faeces |

## the system per dwelling

1. **two bins** — one filling, one curing; each at least 1 m³
2. **layers** — green (fresh, wet) and brown (dry, woody) by volume 1:2; a handful of soil or finished compost per layer
3. **moisture** — damp as a wrung sponge; covered against rain
4. **heat** — 55 °C for three days in a row kills weed seed and pathogens; turn the pile each week while it heats
5. **cure** — two to three months in the second bin; ready when dark, crumbly, earth-smelling
6. **biochar** — charge biochar in compost for a month before it goes into the soil

## where it goes

finished compost goes to the plot's own garden first. surplus goes to the estate nursery through the recycling hub, and is logged there by weight.

## blackwater

human waste goes to the plot's biodigester under the [[design code]]. digestate from the biodigester feeds trees and ornamentals; the compost bins feed the food garden.

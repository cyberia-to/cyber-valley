# recycling

every plot hands over clean, sorted materials; the recycling hub at the mobility hub turns them into resources. this page is the instruction for residents and for the hub.

## on the plot — four streams, clean and dry

| stream | what | how |
|---|---|---|
| glass | bottles, jars | rinsed, lids off, by colour where the hub asks |
| metal | cans, foil, tins, small scrap | rinsed, flattened |
| paper | paper, card, cartons | dry, flat, tape removed |
| plastic | bottles, containers, film | rinsed, dry, by type code 1–7 |

organic waste stays on the plot as [[compost]]. hazardous waste — batteries, lamps, electronics, paint, oil — goes to the hub's locked bay as a fifth stream.

## delivery

residents carry sorted streams to the hub by e-cart or on foot on collection days the register publishes. construction waste leaves the estate by the haul route at the holder's cost.

## at the hub

1. **receive** — accept clean, sorted loads; a mixed load goes back to the sender for sorting
2. **weigh** — log weight per stream and per plot in the ledger
3. **store** — dry, covered bays per stream
4. **process** — glass crushed for aggregate; paper and card shredded for compost and mulch; metal baled; plastic baled by type
5. **dispatch** — bales sold to licensed recyclers; revenue to the estate's operating account
6. **publish** — monthly totals per stream in the register

## burning

open burning of any waste is prohibited under [[air policy]].

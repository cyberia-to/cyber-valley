[[cyberia]] is carbon negative by removing more than it emits, on its own territory, not by offsets bought elsewhere.

## transport

- no petrol or diesel vehicles on estate roads and paths — combustion transport stops at the estate boundary
- internal movement: electric vehicles, e-bikes, bicycles, walking; charging drawn from the estate's own solar/hydro generation, not diesel genset
- exception: construction and heavy-lift machinery without an electric equivalent on the market, for the duration of a specific build only, permitted with 48-hour prior notice to residents within 100 m — the same notice threshold [[air policy]] sets for controlled burns
- no idling: any combustion vehicle or machine on an exception above shuts its engine when not actively working

## combustion and smoke

- no vehicle, generator or machine within estate boundaries may vent exhaust or particulate smoke into occupied or residential zones; the [[air policy]] open-burning and cooking-fire rules apply on top of this, not instead of it
- diesel generators are backup only, tested monthly for no more than 15 minutes, never as primary power

## removal side

- every Plot builds soil carbon under [[soil policy]] rather than depleting it — soil building is counted as removal, not merely as a separate obligation
- [[biochar]] production from estate organic waste is the primary removal pathway; retort/kiln operation follows the [[air policy]] smoke-free secondary-combustion requirement
- forest and canopy cover on the estate is not reduced by construction beyond what [[annex c design code|Annex C]]'s footprint limits already allow

## measurement

- annual accounting: estate-wide emissions (fuel purchased for the exceptions above, backup generator hours) against estimated removal (biochar tonnage, soil carbon trend from periodic testing under [[soil policy]]) — published the same way [[light policy]], [[sound policy]] and [[soil policy]] figures are, once a full year of data exists
- until that first annual account exists, the transport and combustion rules above are the binding standard; the negative-carbon claim is the target the measurement will confirm, not yet a certified fact

## enforcement

Same as [[soil policy]]: first instance — remediation (a combustion vehicle brought back into compliance, or removed from the estate). Second instance — lease review under [[hak sewa deed]] Part 16.

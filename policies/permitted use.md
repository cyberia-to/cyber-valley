# permitted use

which instrument opens which zone to whom. the city amends this page by proposal and vote under [[hak sewa template]] 10.5.

## 1. instruments

| instrument | what it is | contract |
|---|---|---|
| A — [[leasehold upfront]] | 25-year [[hak sewa]], paid in full at signing | [[hak sewa template]], full payment |
| B — [[annual leasehold]] | 25-year hak sewa, a premium at signing and the rest in monthly instalments under the [[century index]] | [[hak sewa template]], century index form |
| C — [[joint venture]] | land contributed to an operating company for 40–60% of its equity | a joint-venture agreement |
| D — [[hak pakai]] | registered right of use in a foreign resident's own name, on a completed house | [[hak sewa template]] 12.1 |
| E — [[hak milik]] | freehold | an AJB before a PPAT |

## 2. who may hold what

| holder | A | B | C | D | E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Indonesian citizen | yes | yes | yes | — | yes |
| foreign individual with KITAS or KITAP | yes | yes | through a PT PMA | yes | — |
| foreign company | through a PT PMA | through a PT PMA | yes | — | — |

E is open to Indonesian citizens alone: the Basic Agrarian Law, UU 5/1960 Pasal 21, reserves hak milik to them. A foreign resident reaches a registered title through D.

## 3. zones

| zone | function | instruments | terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z1 — residences | house plots of 5–15 are | A and B from wave 1; D after SLF; E for citizens | released in waves; wave 1 carries the build obligation |
| Z2 — anchor | spa, market, restaurant, retreat villas | C | the Landowner brings land, the operator brings capital and brand; each deal priced on its own |
| Z3 — community | school, clinic, cowork, common house | land for the community function, rent-free | the land returns to the Landowner when the function ends |
| Z4 — infrastructure | roads, energy, water, data, mobility hub | parking slots under 25-year slot leases | infrastructure is the Landowner's by design |
| Z5 — innovation | labs, studios, startup plots | B at reduced rent against 2–5% equity; C for larger operators | chosen for fit with the Cyberia stack |
| Z6 — agroforestry | food forest, livestock, nursery | B, or an operator agreement | output feeds Z2 |
| Z7 — conservation | watershed, biodiversity | none | income from carbon and biodiversity credits and naming rights |
| Z8 — commons | trails, plazas, public space | none | residents enter under the service charge; visitors buy an entry ticket |

## 4. land-time

the area revenue charge on land-time lettings under [[hak sewa template]] 5.1 is 10% of revenue, or 5% on a plot its holder proves to be a net exporter of energy, water and food under 5.3 of that deed.

| item | rule |
|---|---|
| revenue | the gross amount received for bare-land occupation in the month, before deductions |
| reporting | the holder reports land-time revenue monthly with the invoice for the charge; the Company may check any of the prior 24 months |
| net-exporter plot | the holder meters its plot's energy and water, keeps a food-harvest log and publishes both monthly in the register; the 5% rate starts with the invoice after 12 months of published surplus in all three |
| understatement | the shortfall is paid on demand with [20]% of it added |

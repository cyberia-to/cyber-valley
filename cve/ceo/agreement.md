# CEO agreement — terms

> Draft for counsel · commercial source of truth: [[cve/ceo/offer]] · economics: [[cve/ceo/economics]]  
> Replaces the long [[ceo agreement terms]] draft.

**Parties.** PT Cyber Valley Estate («Company») · CEO / Director (to be named) · Commissioners as approval gate.

---

## 1. Role

CEO is responsible for **operational and strategic management** of the Company: grow a **network of cities in Indonesia**, starting from **~37 ha** on **Sanghyang** (Bali North Star / Cyber Valley) — nature × technology, mountain tourism + high-quality living environment as a tech hub.

**Success metric.** Capitalise and decentralise ownership so **founder (DS + AV) residual ≈ 10%**, on the valuation ladder in [[cve/ceo/economics]]. No other KPI stack.

---

## 2. Dual accounts

| account | holds | CEO may |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategic** | all **incoming revenue**; strategic reserve | propose use; spend only under §3 |
| **Operational** | ops budget (payroll, suppliers, day-to-day) | spend **inside** the approved monthly envelope |

Revenue → **strategic** only. No mixing without a recorded transfer under §4.

---

## 3. Strategic gate (monthly)

Once per month (or written circular):

1. CEO tables **proposals** (funds, material contracts, capital, land above ops norms, anything outside the ops envelope).  
2. **One commissioner** approval is enough.  
3. Day-to-day ops **inside** the envelope needs no commissioner sign-off.

---

## 4. Ops funding (monthly)

- **One** transfer strategic → operational per month.  
- Amount = envelope set in the §3 pack.  
- Overspend or new lines → back to §3.

---

## 5. Land and credit — hard limits

| rule | |
|---|---|
| **Below market** | Any **HGB** or **hak sewa** below market needs **commissioner** approval |
| **Market floor** | **USD 3,000 / are (sotka)** — under this = below market unless commissioner signs |
| **No mortgage** | CEO **cannot** pledge or mortgage Company (or controlled SPV) **real estate** for loans |

Breach of §5 is Cause for removal (with counsel’s closed list of other Cause items).

---

## 6. Economics

All figures — options, KPI ladder, CEO package cut — live only in [[cve/ceo/economics]].  
This agreement does not restate percentages.

---

## 7. Term and exit (thin)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Term** | as in appointment resolution / Articles |
| **Bad Leaver** | closed list: serious fiduciary breach; crime harming the Company; material breach of Articles/SHA/this agreement uncured 30 days after notice |
| **Good Leaver** | everything else (incl. resignation, non-renewal, removal without Cause) |
| **Equity on exit** | Good Leaver keeps shares / ladder equity already earned; Bad Leaver buyback at acquisition cost where SHA so provides |
| **Earned ladder** | CEO package equity already vested from closed KPI sales survives departure |

Detail of put/call with shareholders: [[shareholders agreement]] — keep only what does not defeat Good Leaver rights.

---

## 8. What is **not** in this agreement

No land commission grid, no sub-project referral stack, no investment-raise %, no DevelopmentCo menu, no multi-page indemnification novel.  
If needed later → one-page annex, not a second contract.

# CEO offer — PT Cyber Valley Estate

> Status: **offer for discussion** · not a contract · English for the pack, figures as agreed with founders  
> Next: turn this into the thin set of binding docs (appointment, dual accounts, options, KPI schedule)  
> Supersedes the bulk of the negotiating draft in [[ceo agreement terms]] / [[ceo compensation schedule]] for commercial terms; those pages stay as historical legal draft until counsel rewrites them short.

## Who and why

**Company.** PT Cyber Valley Estate (PMA).

**Mandate.** Hire a CEO who can **grow a network of cities in Indonesia**, starting from the asset the company already holds.

**Today.** Consolidated **~37 ha** on volcano **Sanghyang** (Bali): build **Bali North Star / Cyber Valley** — autonomous city marrying nature and technology. Growth centre for **mountain tourism** and a **high-quality life environment** that can become a technology hub for Indonesia.

**Job in one line.** Capitalise the structure and **decentralise ownership** so founders land at **~10%**, with capital split into an Indonesian block, a foreign (Cyberia holding) block, and a residual for founders + CEO — without wrecking valuation.

---

## 2. Dual accounts

| account | purpose | who moves money |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategic** | All **incoming revenue**; strategic reserve and capital decisions | Strategic rules + commissioner gate (§3) |
| **Operational** | Day-to-day spend (ops, payroll, suppliers) | CEO within ops budget |

**Flow.** Revenue lands on **strategic**. Ops is funded by a **monthly transfer** from strategic → operational (§4). No silent mixing of the two.

---

## 3. Strategic control (monthly)

Once per month (or by written circular):

- CEO tables **proposals** (props) for strategic use of funds and material decisions  
- **One commissioner** approval is enough to pass (not full board theatre)  
- Scope of “strategic” includes: large contracts, capital structure, land deals above ops norms, anything outside the approved monthly ops envelope  

Day-to-day ops inside the envelope does **not** need a commissioner each time.

---

## 4. Operational funding (monthly)

- **Once per month**, a single payment **strategic → operational** for the next period  
- Amount and envelope set in the monthly strategic pack (§3)  
- CEO spends ops account inside that envelope; overspend or new lines go back to §3

## 5. Land and credit hard limits

| rule | detail |
|---|---|
| **Below-market land** | Any **HGB** deal or **hak sewa** at **below market** needs **commissioner approval** |
| **Market floor** | Market reference for this offer: **USD 3,000 per are (sotka)** · deals under this floor = “below market” unless commissioner signs |
| **No mortgage** | CEO has **no power to pledge / mortgage any real estate** of the company (or controlled SPVs) to secure loans |

These are **red lines**, not guidelines.

## 7. KPI — sell DS + AV, exponential valuation ladder

### What counts

| counts toward KPI | does **not** count |
|---|---|
| Sale / transfer of **DS** and **AV** founder shares | Printing **new** share classes for free |
| Cash and value on those sales | “Cash-out” that is only recap theatre without real third-party price |
| | Capital increase where money is paid in but **does not** move DS/AV at a real valuation for this ladder |

KPI = **real distribution of founder equity** at **real prices**, until founders sit at ~**10%**.

### CEO cut on each sale (in the numbers, not a footnote)

On every qualifying sale of DS/AV shares **except the seed step**:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Package** | founders transfer **step size** of company equity in that deal |
| **CEO** | gets **10% of the package** → equity, not cash (`CEO step = 0.10 × step`) |
| **Buyers** | get **90% of the package** and pay cash (`buyer step = 0.90 × step`) |
| **Cash (step)** | **buyer step × valuation** = `0.90 × step × val` |
| **Valuation floor** | company valuation on the deal sheet ≥ table below |

**Seed (1% @ $4M):** no CEO cut — 100% of the package to the buyer, cash = `1% × val`. Not part of the CEO ladder.

**First CEO sale:** the **10%** row — valuation floor **$10M**. Commission and cash rules above apply from that step on.

CEO equity from the ladder is **separate from options (§6)**. Options are extra.

### Target end-state of ownership

| block | target hands |
|---|---|
| **Package 1** | **Indonesian** holders (buyer side of steps) |
| **Package 2** | **Foreign** holders via **Cyberia holding** (buyer side) |
| **Residual ~10%** | **Founders** (DS+AV after ~90% sold) |
| **CEO** | **~8.9%** from ladder cuts (table; no cut on seed) **+** options up to 10% @ $4M (§6) |

Exact split of Package 1 vs 2 is a living capitalisation plan; invariant: founders → **~10%**, with valuation discipline.

### Exponential ladder → **$1B**

Each step after the seed is **+10 pp** of DS+AV sold (not “90% in one go”).

| col | meaning |
|---|---|
| **sold cum.** | cumulative DS+AV **package** sold (founders → buyers+CEO) |
| **step** | package size this step |
| **val** | min **company** valuation |
| **CEO step** | `10% × step` — CEO equity **this step** |
| **CEO cum.** | cumulative CEO equity from the ladder |
| **cash step** | external cash = `90% × step × val` |

| sold cum. | step | val (USD) | **CEO step** | **CEO cum.** | **cash step** (USD) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **1%** | 1% | **4,000,000** | **—** | **0%** | **40,000** |
| **10%** | 9% | **6,000,000** | **0.90%** | **0.90%** | **486,000** |
| **20%** | 10% | **10,000,000** | **1.00%** | **1.90%** | **900,000** |
| **30%** | 10% | **18,000,000** | **1.00%** | **2.90%** | **1,620,000** |
| **40%** | 10% | **30,000,000** | **1.00%** | **3.90%** | **2,700,000** |
| **50%** | 10% | **50,000,000** | **1.00%** | **4.90%** | **4,500,000** |
| **60%** | 10% | **100,000,000** | **1.00%** | **5.90%** | **9,000,000** |
| **70%** | 10% | **200,000,000** | **1.00%** | **6.90%** | **18,000,000** |
| **80%** | 10% | **400,000,000** | **1.00%** | **7.90%** | **36,000,000** |
| **90%** | 10% | **1,000,000,000** | **1.00%** | **8.90%** | **90,000,000** |

**Seed:** no CEO cut · cash **$40k** (full 1% × $4M).  

**Check on the 50% row:** package **10%** @ $50M → CEO **1%** · buyers **9%** · cash **$4.5M**.  

**At 90% sold:** founders residual **~10%** · CEO ladder **8.90%** · buyers **~81.1%** (Package 1+2).

**How to read.**  
- Early (1–30%): soft proof. Mid: real capital. Late → **$1B**.  
- Intermediate closes: log-interpolate **val** between rows; CEO cut **10% of package** from the 10% step on (not on seed).  
- Miss the val floor → step does not count (or founders waive in writing).
